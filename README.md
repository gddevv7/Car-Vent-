# 2017 Toyota RAV4 Dashboard Defrost Air Deflector

A custom 3D-printed airflow deflector designed for a **2017 Toyota RAV4**. Modeled in **Blender**, this part sits inside the upper center dashboard recess to redirect cabin airflow upward toward the windshield, serving as a practical, low-cost fix for a broken factory defroster system.

---

## The Problem & Solution
* **The Problem:** The factory windshield defroster stopped directing air properly, leaving the windshield prone to fogging and condensation during cold or rainy weather.
* **The Solution:** Instead of tearing apart the dashboard for a costly HVAC blend-door repair, I measured the upper center dashboard vent opening and modeled a custom drop-in air scoop in **Blender**. The deflector captures airflow from the center dash outlet and channels it directly onto the windshield.

  [!TIP]
> Use a heat tolerant material/filament such as PETG, ABS, or PC! PLA and standard TPU will get soft! If you must use PLA or TPU increase the thickness.

## Key Features
* **Custom Fit:** Contoured to sit securely inside the upper center dashboard recess of a 2017 Toyota RAV4.
* **Directed Airflow:** Angled internal geometry guides air upward across the glass to clear fog efficiently.

## Lessons Learned * Future Iterations 
* Originally I printed it out of PLA with thin walls and the piece melted because that's all I had, alongside TPU. I then switched to TPU 95A which held up much better and I increased the thickness of the walls as well.
* I really wanted to make a clip that would attach to the vent instead of it being glued down to the car, which is pretty invasive. I couldn't really figure out how to do so without it being significantly in the way of the air flowing. My approach would be like a chip bag clip design and would snap on.
